01 / ABOUT

ENTER THE WORLD OF CYBER SECURITY

CYBER SPACE 2026 is a one-day inter-college event designed to introduce students to the world of Cyber Security, Ethical Hacking, Digital Threats and Cybersecurity Careers.

The event focuses on awareness, understanding and exploration, giving students an opportunity to learn from cybersecurity professionals and discover how they can begin their own journey in the field.

INTRODUCTION TO THE DARK WEB

BEYOND THE SURFACE WEB

SURFACE WEB

Publicly accessible websites indexed by search engines.

DEEP WEB

Content that is not normally indexed by search engines.

DARK WEB

A deliberately hidden part of the internet requiring specific technologies or configurations to access.
